    Chapter 02 — The Signature

    Every look has a signature.

    Your style should feel like yours.

    A haircut is never just maintenance. It is the line people remember — the silhouette that enters a room half a second before you do. That is the idea this website is built around: hair and beauty treated as personal expression, shaped around one person at a time.

    Not a catalogue of looks — a signature. Developed with you, and unmistakably yours.

    Brand direction proposed for the owner's approval — an aspiration, not an existing claim.

    Four crafts. One signature.

    Hair for everyone, beauty, nails and slow-down care — the four proposed chapters this design is built around.
